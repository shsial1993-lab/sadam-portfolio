Featured research direction

From 3D scene understanding to underwater perception

Reliable open-vocabulary 3D scene understanding for robotic perception in terrestrial and underwater environments

This proposed research direction explores combining vision-language models with 3D spatial representations to support object retrieval and scene understanding over time. An underwater setting introduces additional challenges—including light attenuation, scattering, reduced visibility, and limited GPS availability—that make robust perception and uncertainty-aware mapping especially important. This is a research direction, not a claim of a completed system.

01 / MULTIMODAL ALIGNMENTConnect visual-language features with 3D points and object-level map representations.
02 / TEMPORAL CONSISTENCYStudy how scene observations can be associated across views and repeated visits.
03 / ROBUST PERCEPTIONEvaluate uncertainty and domain shift in low-visibility environments.
